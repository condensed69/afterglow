# stripper-dance
2D web game: tip the stripper to show her tits

## Help & tooltips

Card-based surfaces (club buildings, upgrades, research, perks, managers, crew jobs) show their description directly under the name — no `?` icon. The inline help `?` appears only where the tooltip adds information the label does not show: Ledger resources and stats. Hover (or focus) the `?` for the plain-English definition.

## Second room (0.11.1)

After your **first franchise deal** (prestige) **and** at least one manager, the header shows **Open second room** — confirm to unlock the annex, a second club with its own cash, crowd, buildings, and shift clock. Clout, Legacy, research, crew, and managers stay shared across both rooms.

- **Switch rooms** with the `[ Main ] [ Annex ]` buttons in the header; the inactive room pauses (no offline progress while away).
- The **Ledger** labels the active room and shows its resources; Clout/Legacy are account-wide.
- **Crew is shared**: a room's Dressing Rooms cap how many crew can work there — excess crew are parked off shift when you switch, and stay parked until reassigned (Crew tab).

## Renown, Brand perks & the Rooftop (0.11.10)

Max **every prestige perk**, hire **every manager**, and own **both rooms** — the Perks panel then gains **Sell the franchise**: a bigger reset (everything wipes except Renown, achievements, and Brand ranks) in exchange for **Renown**.

- **Renown** (shown as `N spare · M lifetime` on the Perks panel) buys **Brand perks**: Nationwide Reach (+10% all cash per rank), Loyalty Program (start each run with +1 Regular per rank), R&D Lab (−10% research cost per rank), Night Owl Network (+10% offline per rank), and **Rooftop Lease** — which unlocks the **Rooftop**, a third room with its own till, crowd, and build stack (open it from the Perks panel after buying the lease).
- Each room has its own **location extras**: the Neon Pool in the Main Room, the Rooftop Bar + Skyline View in the Annex, and the Helipad Lounge + Panorama Deck on the Rooftop — bought from the same Structures/Upgrades tabs, alongside the shared catalog.
- Switch rooms with the header buttons; Brand ranks survive selling the franchise — they are the reason to build and sell again.
